The House of SKY FLORALS

Quiet Sophistication & Heritage

Born From Reverence For Creation

SKY FLORALS was born from a profound admiration for the beauty of creation and the belief that flowers are among the greatest masterpieces ever designed. To us, every bloom is a work of art—thoughtfully created with purpose, character, and extraordinary beauty.

Drawing aesthetic inspiration from world-renowned floral heritage—from the haute couture ateliers of Paris to the renaissance artistic balance of Florence—we serve clients across New Jersey, New York, and our atelier in Loja, Ecuador.

“At SKY FLORALS, every arrangement begins with purpose—not with petals. Every creation is interpreted through design, guided by emotion, and finished with quiet sophistication.”
